Akthar al-fidayo tatajayaz fi thalath thawani. Ana a'mal al-fidayo allati yatafarraj al-nas 'alayha hatta al-nihaya. Ana Ubaid — muhtarif fit'haat al-video al-mustahdafa, wa kitabat al-sinario, wa otomasyon YouTube. Fi akthar min ithnayn min al-sanawaat, sallamtu aktar min alf mashroua, li-aktar min khams mi'a zawen sa'udayn, min kulli makaan. Wa al-aan, ana wa team-i na'mal b-itizam ma'a al-markat wa al-qanawat hawla al-'alam. Min montajat YouTube al-fayres wa reels al-tasawul, ila i'laanaat al-a'maal wa motion graphics allati tuhawwil — ana aakhud fikratak min al-sinario al-kham ila silah al-mar'iy al-kamil. Al-tahrir al-jayyid yajlib al-mushahadat. Al-tahrir al-'azeem yajlib al-nata'ij. Tafaddal bi-mushahadat 'amali taht. Wa 'indama takun jaahiz… hayya nabda' shay'an la yomkin ahad an yatajahalahu.
